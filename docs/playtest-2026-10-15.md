# Playtest 2026-10-15 — adversarial, the court and the Lists

The Ojjul Nar Combine, 12 turns, played by the adversarial-player agent through
`scripts/pt.mjs` against `main` at 1c8d184 (#63 and #64 merged). About $11 of
model spend. The subscription hit its session limit midway through turn 10:
from then on every arbiter call failed, and turns 10–11 are thin. End Turn
still went through, with `reactions: []` and nothing to say the reactions had
failed. The fallback epilogue worked. Final position: 6 worlds (Hollow Star and
Var Hollow taken), 5,795 credits, dissent 9.

The raw responses are in `saves/pt-out-*.json` (gitignored) and the session is
in `saves/pt-ojjul.json`.
