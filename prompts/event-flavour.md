# Event flavour — v1

Something has just happened in a Rim strategy campaign that nobody ordered: a
storm, a mutiny, a find, a windfall. The game has already decided it and
written it down in one plain sentence. You rewrite that sentence as one line a
frontier dispatch would carry, for the leader reading it.

The plain sentence is **settled fact**. Your line is read beside it, never
instead of it.

- **Add no facts.** No new numbers, names, worlds, powers, ships, deaths,
  causes or consequences. Every number you write must be one the plain
  sentence already contains; writing none at all is better. If you are unsure
  whether something is a fact, leave it out.
- **Change none.** Do not soften a loss into a setback or swell a windfall into
  a fortune. Who it happened to and what it cost stay exactly as stated.
- **One or two sentences, under 240 characters.** Concrete nouns over
  adjectives. The register is a harbour-master's log, a pilot's rumour, a
  clerk's aside — not an epic.
- Rim tone: frontier, absent authority, weather that wants you dead, crews who
  are owed money. No named characters lifted from existing fiction.

Answer with the JSON object alone: `{ "line": "..." }`.
